## 📌 SLIDE 4: FEASIBILITY AND VIABILITY

### 1. TOP HEADER & BRANDING
- **Top Left Circle**: `MedRelay` *(Replaces VitalNode)*
- **Main Title**: **FEASIBILITY AND VIABILITY**
- **Sub-heading**: **Grounded in What Already Works**

---

### 2. LEFT TOP: "WHY IT IS FEASIBLE" (4 Bullet Points)
- **Built on ABDM & Government Standards**: Integrates with existing ABHA IDs, HPR, and HFR registries; no new ID system required.
- **Offline-First Architecture**: Uses low-bandwidth LocalStorage sync & Base64 compressed voice notes proven in 2G rural environments.
- **Gamified ASHA Empowerment**: Frontline ASHA workers (Swasthya Mitras) act as onboarders with points/DBT incentives rather than digital barriers.
- **Phased Operational Launch**: Modular deployment starting with PHC OPD queues & emergency SOS before full district command rollout.

---

### 3. LEFT BOTTOM: "PHASED ROLLOUT" (3 Connected Boxes)
- **Box 1**: `Phase 1: PHC Pilot (Villianur & Bahour)`
- **Box 2**: `Phase 2: Puducherry District Scale`
- **Box 3**: `Phase 3: State & UT Expansion`

---

### 4. RIGHT SIDE: "RISKS & HOW WE ADDRESS THEM" (5 Row Matrix)

| Risk Category | Mitigation Strategy |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Low Digital Literacy** | Voice-first Web Speech API & dedicated ASHA Worker SOS Portal (`/asha`) for assisted entry. |
| **Patchy Rural Connectivity** | Offline-first event bus with automatic background sync when 2G/4G re-establishes. |
| **Health Data Privacy** | ABDM-compliant consent management, explicit ABHA user authorization, and encrypted FHIR R4 bundles. |
| **Hospital & 108 Buy-in** | District health department MoUs and unified dual-hub dispatch dashboard for IGGGH Pondicherry. |
| **Liability of AI Guidance** | AI provides clinical decision support only; final diagnosis & prescriptions remain with certified doctors. |
